# Business Findings & Recommendations

## Executive findings

- **Revenue:** ₹11.61M across 60,000 transactions and 161,511 units.
- **Customers:** 7,993 customers made at least one purchase.
- **Category leader:** Juices generated about ₹3.08M and were the highest-revenue category.
- **Volume leader:** Carbonated Drinks sold the most units, while Juices led revenue.
- **Regional leader:** South generated about ₹2.85M, the highest regional revenue.
- **Channel leader:** Supermarkets generated about ₹5.31M, the largest channel contribution.
- **Customer value:** Premium customers had the highest average spend per customer at about ₹1,464, only slightly above Mass Market.

## Category growth

2025 vs 2024 revenue growth:

| Category | Growth |
|---|---:|
| Juices | +2.15% |
| Carbonated Drinks | +1.72% |
| Packaged Water | -0.17% |
| Sports Drinks | -0.20% |
| Energy Drinks | -0.65% |

### Recommendation
Protect and expand the Juice portfolio, especially in regions where it already leads. Review Energy Drink assortment, pricing, and promotion because it showed the largest year-over-year decline.

## Regional opportunity

South is the strongest region overall. Juices lead revenue in five of the six regions, while Energy Drinks lead in West.

### Recommendation
Use localized category planning rather than one national assortment: prioritize Juice availability and activation broadly, while testing stronger Energy Drink propositions in West.

## Channel strategy

Supermarkets drive scale. Direct has the highest average transaction value but much smaller volume.

### Recommendation
Use supermarkets for broad reach and portfolio visibility. Treat Direct as a higher-value relationship channel where retention, bundles, or premium offers can be tested.

## Consumer targeting

Age and income show weak linear relationships with total spend in this synthetic dataset. Customer segment, channel, product, and region are more actionable dimensions for the dashboard story.

### Recommendation
Avoid claiming that higher income automatically means higher spend. Build targeting around observed purchase behavior, category preference, channel preference, and regional demand.

## Data-quality caveat

The synthetic transaction structure produces a very high repeat-purchase rate (99.74%). This is not used as a headline business KPI because it is structurally inflated by the simulated data-generation process.

The market benchmark is also synthetic. Any benchmark-derived market share should be explicitly labeled as synthetic rather than presented as real market share.
